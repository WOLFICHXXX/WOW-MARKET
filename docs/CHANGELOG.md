# WolfichMarket changelog

Each release takes its notes from the matching "## version" section below; they appear on CurseForge as the file's changelog.

## 2.43.37

### ✨ New (trial)
- **Own crafting window:** when a profession opens, the addon's Professions section replaces the game window. *Create xN* crafts right from here, *All (N)* - as many as the materials in bags allow. *Game window* brings back the usual window until the next opening; can be turned off in Settings.

### ✨ Новое (проба)
- **Своё окно крафта:** при открытии профессии вместо окна игры - раздел «Профессии» аддона. *Создать ×N* крафтит прямо отсюда, *Все (N)* - сколько хватит материалов в сумках. *Окно игры* вернёт обычное окно до следующего открытия; выключается в настройках.

## 2.43.36

**🇬🇧 English** · 🇷🇺 Русский - ниже

### ✨ New minimalist look
- **Compact main window:** a narrow strip of simple section icons on the left (house, characters, flask, gavel, hourglass, gear; names on hover), a thin header with the section name and the cloth background with paws. The window is noticeably smaller. The old one: *Settings → Compact main window*, then reload the UI.
- **Flat buttons everywhere:** dark fill with a thin gold edge, the main or selected one gold, labels exactly centered. Checkboxes stay as before.
- **Auctioneer bag:** a minimalist frame, the slots as before, the character's money with coin icons at the bottom right.
- **Game profession window:** the *where to buy and where to sell* block and the F / N profit columns in the new style.

### 🔨 Auction
- **Buy tab:** a search box and a category picker - items from the latest snapshot, cheapest first.
- **Plan:** change the count of every recipe with **-** / **+** or type it in.

### 🛠 Fixes
- Usable bag items (bedroll and others) are no longer blocked after visiting the auction house.
- **Refresh prices** no longer sends a query while the auction house has not answered the previous one - the game window no longer hangs on *"Searching..."*, and refreshing is faster.
- Posting waits until the game knows whether an item is a commodity - such lots used to hang the auction house; no false *"not responding"* right after opening.

## 2.43.26

### 🛠 Fixes
- **Refresh prices** no longer sends a query while the auction house has not answered the previous one - this used to keep it busy and the game window stuck on *"Searching..."*. The next query goes out as soon as the auction house is free, so refreshing is faster.
- **Posting:** if the auction house stays busy for long, the lot is still sent on click; the false *"not responding"* right after opening is gone.

### 🛠 Исправлено
- **«Обновить цены»** больше не шлёт запрос, пока аукцион не ответил на прошлый - раньше это держало аукцион занятым и окно игры зависало на *«Поиск...»*. Следующий запрос уходит сразу, как аукцион освободился, - обновление быстрее.
- **Выставление:** если аукцион долго «занят», лот всё равно отправляется по нажатию; убрано ложное *«Аукцион не отвечает»* сразу после открытия.

## 2.42.0

- Shared market: when someone's snapshot arrives in full, prices that did not change get the time of that snapshot, so they no longer look old.
- A snapshot is sent even when no prices changed: it then carries only a time confirmation.
- Items that disappeared from the auction house are removed for everyone who receives the snapshot.

## 2.41.7

Everything since 2.21.2:

- Shared market: the whole auction house is shared, with alerts and recipes first. Turbo mode, dense price packing, only changed prices are sent, several scanners take turns, a newcomer gets the whole auction personally.
- Safe traffic limit: no more disconnects after a scan (WOW51900319).
- Alerts with your own price: "buy at or below" and "sell at or above" instead of percentages.
- Two auction houses: faction and neutral (Zephyr Isle, Stranglethorn Vale, Tanaris), detected by zone and kept separately, with their own fees (5% and 15%). The Riverglades auction is ignored.
- Main window "Auctions" tab: the whole faction auction with sorting and categories (including profession crafted items / materials); for the neutral auction - what is better to buy or sell there.
- Profession window: crafting profit in F / N columns, a "where to buy and where to sell" table, sorting recipes by profit, "Add to plan" button.
- Plan: "Plan" button on the auctioneer's Craft tab, a hint where each material is cheapest.
- Price chart: a second line with sold quantity; the recipe card scrolls down to a large chart.
- Bags: icons on items (gavel - auction, coin bag - vendor), sorting puts auction items on top and vendor items at the bottom, "All" quantity button when selling.
- Item tooltip: crafting cost, auction price and profit.
- Posting checked items waits for the game to confirm each lot and no longer hangs the auction house.
- Professions are re-read when the skill grows or new recipes are learned.
